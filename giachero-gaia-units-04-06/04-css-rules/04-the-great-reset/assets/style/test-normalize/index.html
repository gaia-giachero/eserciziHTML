PRAGUE

3 Days in Prague

DAY 1 - ARRIVAL AND OLD TOWN

Departure from Turin and arrival in Prague (by plane is the easiest way). Go to the hotel and check in.

AFTERNOON

  • Old Town Square - The main square of the city. Very beautiful.
  • Prague Astronomical Clock – Every hour you can see a short show with moving statues.
  • Old Town Hall Tower – You can go up for a nice view of the city.

Sunset: walk on Charles Bridge. It is one of the most famous places in Prague. It is very nice at sunset.

EVENING

Dinner in a traditional restaurant in Staré Město (Old Town).

DAY 2 - CASTLE AND HISTORIC AREAS

MORNING: CASTLE AREA

  • Prague Castle – One of the biggest castles in the world.
  • St. Vitus Cathedral – A big and beautiful church inside the castle.
  • Golden Lane – Small and colorful houses.

AFTERNOON

  • Walk in Malá Strana. It is a quiet and romantic area.
  • Go up to Petřín Hill for a beautiful view. You can take the funicular.

EVENING

Have dinner with typical Czech food and local beer like U Tri Pstrosu

DAY 3 - JEWISH QUARTER AND MODERN AREA

MORNING

  • Visit Josefov, the Jewish Quarter.
  • Old Jewish Cemetery – Very interesting and historic place.
  • Spanish Synagogue – Very beautiful inside.

AFTERNOON

  • Walk in Wenceslas Square. It is a modern and busy area.
  • Free time for shopping or visit a museum.

EVENING

Go to the airport and fly back to Turin.
