LONDON

4 Days in London

ITINERARY

DAY 1 - CENTRAL LONDON

After arriving from Turin by bus and leaving your luggage at the hotel:

  • Big Ben – The symbol of London
  • Palace of Westminster – The Parliament (next to Big Ben)
  • Westminster Abbey – A very famous historic church
  • Walk to Buckingham Palace – The King’s residence
  • Relax in St. James's Park

Afternoon:

  • London Eye – Beautiful view of the city
  • Evening walk in Piccadilly Circus

Evening:

  • Walk around Piccadilly Circus and enjoy the lights.
  • Explore Leicester Square (cinemas, shops, street performers).
  • Optional: quick visit to China Town for a snack or dinner.

DAY 2 - HISTORY AND VIEWS

Morning:

  • Visit the Tower of London
  • Walk on Tower Bridge

Walk along the River Thames.

Afternoon:

  • Visit St. Paul's Cathedral
  • Go up to The Shard for a great view

Evening:

  • Walk around Covent Garden after dinner and enjoy the street performers.
  • Explore the small streets nearby for shops and cafés.
  • Relax with a drink in a cozy pub or take a short walk to Trafalgar Square to see it lit up at night.

DAY 3 - MUSEUMS AND FAMOUS AREAS

Morning:

  • Visit the British Museum (free entry)

    • or

  • Visit the Natural History Museum

Afternoon:

  • Walk in Camden Town (markets and alternative style)

    • or

  • Shopping on Oxford Street

Evening:

  • Relax in Soho, a lively area with many cafés and restaurants.
  • Short walk to Carnaby Street for shops and music vibes.
  • End the day with a drink in a cozy pub nearby.

DAY 4 – NOTTING HILL, KENSINGTON AND SOUTH BANK

Morning:

  • Notting Hill
    • Walk through the colorful streets.
    • Explore Portobello Road Market (vintage items, antiques, street food).
    • Have a simple breakfast in a small café.
  • Kensington
    • Relax in Kensington Gardens.
    • Optional visit to Kensington Palace.

Afternoon:

  • Visit the Victoria & Albert Museum (art and design)

    • or

  • Visit the Science Museum (fun and interactive).

Evening:

  • Walk along the River Thames.
  • Enjoy the view of the London Eye at sunset.
  • Dinner in a restaurant or pub by the river.
